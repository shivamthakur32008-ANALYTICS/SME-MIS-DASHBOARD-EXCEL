# 📊 SME MIS Dashboard - Excel

> A comprehensive Management Information System (MIS) Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel for Small & Medium Enterprises (SMEs). Consolidates key business metrics into an interactive, real-time dashboard for data-driven decision making.

---

## 🎯 Overview

SME owners and managers need quick access to critical business metrics without spending hours in spreadsheets. This MIS dashboard provides:

- **At-a-glance KPI cards** showing business health
- **Real-time performance metrics** updated automatically
- **Interactive visualizations** revealing patterns and trends
- **Smart filtering** for detailed drill-down analysis
- **Professional formatting** ready for board meetings

**Goal:** Transform data chaos into business clarity.

## 🚀 How to Use

### Phase 1: Setup
1. **Prepare your data** — Organize in consistent format
2. **Input source data** into the data entry sheet
3. **Configure formulas** — Link to your data sources
4. **Customize metrics** — Add/modify KPIs for your business

### Phase 2: Analysis
1. **Review KPI Cards** — Understand current performance
2. **Analyze Trends** — Chart patterns over time
3. **Use Slicers** — Filter by dimension for detailed views
4. **Compare Periods** — Month-over-month or year-over-year
5. **Identify Issues** — Spot underperformance or anomalies

### Phase 3: Decision Making

## 💡 Real-World Use Case

**Scenario:** An SME manufacturer has revenue across 3 product lines, 5 regions, and multiple departments. CEO needs answers:
- "Are we on track to hit our annual target?"
- "Which region is underperforming?"
- "Where can we cut costs?"
- "Which product should we focus on?"
- "Is profit growing with revenue?"

**Solution:** This MIS Dashboard provides instant answers:
- **KPI Cards** show revenue $2.3M (95% of target)
- **Regional Chart** reveals North region down 12%
- **Cost Breakdown** shows logistics is 35% of COGS
- **Product Mix** shows Product A contributes 60% revenue
- **Trend Analysis** shows margins shrinking due to pricing pressure

**Result:** Management makes informed decisions backed by data, not guesswork.
